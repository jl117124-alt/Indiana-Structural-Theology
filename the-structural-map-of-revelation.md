# THE STRUCTURAL MAP OF REVELATION
(Christ-Centered, Two-Horizon, Recapitulating)


#### I. PROLOGUE & THRONE (Rev 1–5)
1. Christ Among the Lampstands (Rev 1)

   - Risen Christ.
   - Present with churches.
   - Already enthroned authority.

Not future.
Already reigning.

2. Letters to Seven Churches (Rev 2–3)

   -   Real first-century congregations.
   -   Real persecution.
   -   Real compromise issues.
   -   “I am coming soon” (immediacy layer).

This grounds the book in first-century reality.

3. Throne Room & Scroll (Rev 4–5)

   -   Father on throne.
   -   Scroll of judgment.
   -   Lamb worthy.
   -   Authority centralized in Christ.

This establishes:
All judgment in the book proceeds from the enthroned Lamb.
#### II. COVENANT ESCALATION CYCLES (Rev
6–16)
These cycles are not strictly chronological.
They recapitulate escalating covenant judgment.


A. Seals (6–8)
   -   War
   -   Famine
   -   Persecution
   -   Martyrs cry “How long?”
   -   Cosmic disturbance imagery

Matches:
Olivet Discourse covenant-judgment layer.


B. Trumpets (8–11)
   - Partial judgments (1/3 language).
   - Temple imagery (measuring in ch. 11).
   - City where Lord was crucified.

Strong Jerusalem pressure here.


C. Conflict Vision (12–14)
   -   Woman → Child → Dragon.
   -   Child enthroned.
   -   Dragon cast down.
   -   War shifts to saints.

This is interpretive overlay:
Incarnation → Ascension → Church persecution.

Not chronological future.
Theological recapitulation.


D. Bowls (15–16)
   - Full-strength judgments.
   - No more 1/3 restraint.
   - Preparation for collapse of Babylon.

Escalation toward covenant enforcement climax.


#### III. BABYLON COLLAPSE (Rev 17–18)
This is the covenant defendant layer.

Woman = Great City.
Drunk with blood of saints.
Riding Beast (imperial coercion).

Beast turns.
Woman burned.

This fits 70 AD covenant enforcement layer.

Jerusalem as harlot-city under judgment
via imperial machinery.

This is Horizon A.


#### IV. VICTORY HINGE (Rev 19)
   -   Heaven rejoices over Babylon’s fall.
   -   Rider appears (Christ revealed as victorious judge).
   -   Beast and False Prophet seized.
   -   Lake of fire imagery.

This is covenant-vindication victory.

It does not yet include:

   - Universal resurrection.
   - Final courtroom.
   - Abolition of death.
So it functions as:
Christ triumphant over persecuting power.

Hinge scene.


#### V. PANORAMIC CHURCH AGE (Rev
20:1–10)
Recapitulation zoom-out.


A. Binding of Satan
Restrained from deceiving nations.
Explains gospel expansion era.


B. First Resurrection
Souls reigning with Christ.
Present spiritual life.
Parallel to John 5 “now is.”


C. Millennium
Symbolic fullness of church age.
Not literal geopolitical thousand-year regime.


D. Release
Brief final rebellion.
Gog/Magog imagery (Ezekiel recapitulation).
Final suppression.

Still no universal resurrection yet.
#### VI. FINAL UNIVERSAL JUDGMENT (Rev
20:11–15)
Now the clear break.

   -   Earth and sky flee.
   -   Dead small and great stand.
   -   Books opened.
   -   Death and Hades abolished.

This is Horizon B.
Final consummation layer.
Unmistakable.

No collapse with 70 AD possible.


#### VII. NEW CREATION (Rev 21–22)
   -   New heaven and earth.
   -   New Jerusalem (not harlot city; purified bride).
   -   No more death.
   -   No temple (God and Lamb are temple).
   -   River of life.
   -   Confirmed communion.

Telos achieved.

# WHY THIS FRAMEWORK HOLDS
It:

✔ Honors first-century relevance.
✔ Keeps Olivet’s two horizons intact.
✔ Avoids collapsing 70 AD into final judgment.
✔ Avoids futurist speculation.
✔ Keeps Christ central at every stage.
✔ Respects apocalyptic recapitulation pattern.
✔ Aligns with John 5 resurrection categories.


The Cherry on Top

It is:

      -   A covenant lawsuit.
      -   A persecution manual.
      -   A vindication proclamation.
      -   A Christ-exalting apocalypse.
      -   A final judgment assurance.

It begins with:
Christ among His churches.
It ends with:
Christ dwelling with His people forever.

Everything in between
is governed by the Lamb who was slain.

That is the arc.

No drift.
No collapse.
No newspaper prophecy.
No allegorical excess.

Christ enthroned → covenant judged → church preserved → death abolished → God with man.

That is Revelation.

Interpreter complete.
