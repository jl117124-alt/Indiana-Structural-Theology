# **JESUS HERMENEUTIC LENS**

Version 1.0 — Fully Consolidated

---

# **1\. Purpose**

This document identifies the **interpretive operations used by Jesus when reading Scripture**.

The goal is descriptive rather than speculative. The lens observes how Jesus interprets Scripture when:

* teaching disciples  
* confronting opponents  
* declaring fulfillment  
* resolving doctrinal disputes.

These operations form the **foundational hermeneutic framework behind the apostolic interpretive methods**.

---

# **2\. Governing Burden**

Jesus reads Scripture as a **coherent divine witness that converges on the Messiah**, revealing:

* His identity  
* His mission  
* His suffering  
* His vindication  
* His kingdom.

Scripture therefore functions as a **Christ-convergent revelation system fulfilled in Jesus**.

---

# **3\. Foundational Assumptions**

Across the Gospel accounts Jesus consistently assumes:

### **Scripture is unified**

The Law, Prophets, and Writings form one coherent revelation.

### **Scripture testifies about the Messiah**

The canonical witness ultimately points toward Christ.

### **Scripture contains layered meaning**

Meaning often lies deeper than surface interpretation.

### **Prophecy reaches fulfillment in real history**

Prophecy is fulfilled in events centered on Christ.

### **Understanding requires illumination**

Interpretive failure is frequently moral and spiritual.

Institution → fulfillment.

# **4.7 Covenant Concession Recognition**

# **4.8 Interpretive Illumination vs Blindness**
