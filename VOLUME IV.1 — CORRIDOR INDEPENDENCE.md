# **SET IV — CONVERGENCE ARCHITECTURE**

## **VOLUME IV.1 — CORRIDOR INDEPENDENCE**

*(Canonical Form — Anti-Circularity Layer)*

---

## **I. Purpose**

To establish:

**That the Death Corridor, Creation Corridor, and Revelation Corridor are logically independent and do not rely on each other for their derivation**

---

## **II. Boundary**

This document assumes:

* Set I is complete  
* Set II is complete  
* Set III is complete  
* IV.0 interface conditions are defined but not used as a derivation source

---

This document does NOT:

* introduce new constraints  
* identify any subject  
* perform convergence

---

It establishes only:

**non-circularity of the system**

---

## **III. Definition — Corridor Independence**

A corridor is independent if:

**it can be derived without requiring the constraints or conclusions of another corridor**

---

## **IV. Independence of Set I (Death Corridor)**

Set I derives:

* definition of death  
* failure of self-recovery  
* required solution structure

### **Source:**

* analysis of death condition

### **Does NOT require:**

* identity-ground  
* scalar structure  
* revelation

---

## **Result**

**Set I is independent**

---

## **V. Independence of Set II (Creation Corridor)**

Set II derives:

* identity persistence  
* invariance  
* scalar requirement  
* identity-ground

### **Source:**

* analysis of system identity

### **Does NOT require:**

* death problem  
* restoration mechanism  
* revelation

---

## **Result**

**Set II is independent**

---

## **VI. Independence of Set III (Revelation Corridor)**

Set III derives:

* underdetermination of identity  
* necessity of revelation  
* constraints on revelation

### **Source:**

* limitation of derivation

### **Does NOT require:**

* any identified subject  
* any specific revelation claim  
* any convergence result

---

## **Result**

**Set III is independent**

---

## **VII. Non-Circularity**

Circularity would occur if:

* Set I required Set II  
* Set II required Set III  
* Set III required Set I

---

No such dependencies exist.

---

## **VIII. Result**

**All three corridors are independently derived and non-circular**

---

## **IX. Implication**

Because each corridor is independent:

**Any convergence must be established after independence and cannot be assumed within any corridor**

---

## **X. Locked Statement**

**The Death Corridor, Creation Corridor, and Revelation Corridor are independently derived systems whose conclusions do not depend on one another, ensuring that any later convergence is not circular.**

---

## **XI. Boundary Statement**

This document does NOT:

* align constraints  
* perform convergence  
* identify any subject

---

It establishes only:

**independence and non-circularity**
