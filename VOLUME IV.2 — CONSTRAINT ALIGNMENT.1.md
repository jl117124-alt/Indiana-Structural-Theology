# **SET IV — CONVERGENCE ARCHITECTURE**

# **VOLUME IV.2 — CONSTRAINT ALIGNMENT**

*(Canonical Form — Pre-Bridging Layer)*

---

## **I. Purpose**

To establish:

**That the constraints derived from Set I and Set II must be jointly satisfied and can be placed into direct alignment without modification or collapse**

---

## **II. Boundary**

This document assumes:

* Set I is complete  
* Set II is complete  
* Set III is complete  
* IV.0 interface conditions are defined  
* IV.1 independence is established

---

This document does NOT:

* introduce new constraints  
* resolve how constraints are satisfied  
* identify any subject  
* perform bridging

---

It establishes only:

**direct alignment of constraints across independent corridors**

---

## **III. Definition — Constraint Alignment**

Constraint alignment is:

**the placement of independently derived constraints into a single requirement set in which all constraints must hold simultaneously without alteration**

---

Alignment does NOT:

* merge constraints  
* reduce constraints  
* reinterpret constraints

---

It requires:

**full retention of each constraint in its original form**

---

## **IV. Aligned Constraint Set**

---

### **IV.1 Identity Requirement**

From Set I:

* the same person must be restored

From Set II:

* identity is determined by a singular, active ground

---

**Aligned condition:**

The restoration of the same person must occur under a singular identity-determining ground.

---

---

### **IV.2 Continuity Requirement**

From Set I:

* death introduces rupture

From Set II:

* identity continuity is not system-derived

---

**Aligned condition:**

Continuity across death must be preserved independent of the system undergoing rupture.

---

---

### **IV.3 Existence Requirement**

From Set I:

* the same embodied person must be restored

From Set II:

* existence is determined by the same ground as identity

---

**Aligned condition:**

Restoration of the same person requires re-instantiation by the identity–existence ground.

---

---

### **IV.4 Domain Requirement**

From Set I:

* the solution must operate within the human domain

From Set II:

* the ground is non-derived and not system-dependent

---

**Aligned condition:**

The required subject must operate within the human domain while not being system-derived.

---

---

### **IV.5 Non-Duplication Requirement**

From Set I:

* the same person must be restored

From Set II:

* identity is singular and non-duplicable

---

**Aligned condition:**

Restoration cannot involve duplication, substitution, or multiple identity instances.

---

---

### **IV.6 Extension Requirement**

From Set I:

* the solution must extend beyond a single individual

From Set II:

* the ground is singular

---

**Aligned condition:**

Extension must occur without multiplication of the identity ground.

---

---

## **V. Result**

The alignment produces:

* a unified constraint set  
* full retention of all constraints  
* no reduction or collapse

---

The aligned set:

* cannot be satisfied by partial compliance  
* cannot be simplified  
* remains unresolved at the level of mechanism

---

This document:

* does not explain how constraints are satisfied  
* does not introduce a mediating mechanism  
* does not resolve tension between constraints  
* does not identify any subject

---

It establishes only:

**that all constraints must be held together simultaneously**
