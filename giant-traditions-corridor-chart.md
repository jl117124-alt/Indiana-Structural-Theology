Corridor Interpretation
Corridor A — Pre-Flood Anomaly

The Nephilim belong to the Genesis 6 corruption event.
This corridor ends with the Flood.
Corridor B — Post-Flood Giant Peoples

After the Flood, giant traditions appear as large human warrior peoples:

   -   Rephaim
   -   Anakim
   -   Emim
   -   Zamzummim
   -   Og of Bashan
   -   Philistine giant warriors

All remain within ordinary human lineage descending from Noah.


The Overlap Point

Numbers 13:33 creates the only place where the two traditions touch.

The spies describe the Anakim using the legendary Nephilim language.

Because the report is explicitly called a bad report, this reference functions best as fear
rhetoric, not genealogy.


Structural Result
The corridor stabilizes as:

Genesis 6 Nephilim
      ↓
Flood Reset
      ↓
Human Line of Noah
      ↓
Rephaim / Anakim Giant Traditions
      ↓
Philistine Giants (Goliath)


The Nephilim remain a pre-Flood anomaly, and the later giant traditions remain fully human.
Why This Chart Matters
This corridor prevents three common interpretive errors:

    1. Flattening the giant traditions
        (treating every giant as a Nephilim)
    2. Inventing post-Flood hybrids
        (which Scripture never records)
    3. Importing mythological inflation
        into the biblical narrative

Instead, the chart preserves both:

    - the Genesis 6 anomaly, and
    - the historical giant traditions of the Old Testament.
