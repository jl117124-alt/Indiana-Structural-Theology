# **II. DEFINITION — VALID RE-DESCRIPTION**

---

A valid re-description is:

a transformation of representation that does not change the underlying physical situation

---

### **Includes:**

* regrouping  
* decomposition  
* recomposition  
* coordinate transformation  
* basis transformation

---

### **Requirement:**

If two descriptions are valid, they refer to the same system

---

# **IV. NECESSARY CONDITION**

---

**Same-system identity under valid re-description requires an invariant scalar measure**

# **V. REQUIRED PROPERTIES OF THE SCALAR**

---

The scalar must satisfy:

---

### **R1 — Decomposition Invariance**

Breaking into parts preserves total

---

### **R2 — Aggregation Invariance**

Combining parts preserves total

---

### **R3 — Composition Consistency**

\[  
f(A \\cdot B) \= f(A) \\cdot f(B)  
\]

---

### **R4 — Representation Independence**

Equivalent descriptions yield identical scalar values

---

---
