# DOCTRINAL DECLARATION 999
The Christ-Telos Covenant Architecture


Preamble
This declaration articulates a Christ-centered theological architecture in which the
eternal Son is the telos of creation, the Incarnation is not a reaction to the Fall but the
fulfillment of divine purpose, and redemption is the contingent mode by which the
unchanged goal is achieved after rebellion.

This document does not seek novelty.
It seeks structural coherence.

The governing claim is simple:

Christ is the reason for creation.


#### I. The Ultimate Telos
We affirm that all things were created through the Son and for the Son.

Creation does not exist primarily as a testing ground, nor merely as a stage for
redemption, but as the arena in which the eternal purpose of God in Christ is revealed
and consummated.

Christ is not Plan B.
He is Alpha and Omega.


#### II. The Eternal Giving of a People
We affirm that the Father eternally gives a people to the Son.

This giving:
   - Is not contingent upon sin.

   - Is not merely a rescue arrangement.

   - Is grounded in the eternal counsel of the Triune God.


Even in a sinless world, the Father would give a people to the Son.

The gift concerns:

   - Covenant headship,

   - Communion,

   - Creator–creature union.


Redemption does not create this purpose.
Redemption serves it.


#### III. Incarnation as Telos
We affirm that the Incarnation belongs to the eternal purpose of God.

The Word becoming flesh is not merely an emergency response to rebellion, but the
consummation of Creator–creature union.

Two modes must be distinguished:

A. Telos Mode (Sinless Scenario)

In a world without sin, the Incarnation would accomplish:

   - Covenant consummation,

   - Personal union between Creator and creature,

   - Elevation beyond probationary innocence,

   - Confirmed participation in divine life.
No atonement would be required in this mode.

B. Redemptive Mode (Fallen Scenario)

Because rebellion occurred, the Incarnation includes:

   - Fulfillment of all righteousness,

   - Satisfaction of divine justice,

   - Defeat of death,

   - Removal of corruption,

   - Historical revelation of divine mercy and justice.


The goal remains unchanged.
The path is altered by sin.


#### IV. Probation and Glory
We affirm a real distinction between probationary innocence and confirmed glory.

Probationary Innocence

   - Real righteousness,

   - Real alignment,

   - Mutable condition,

   - Vulnerability to deception,

   - Not yet confirmed in covenant headship.


Failure was possible.

Confirmed Glory
   - Righteousness fulfilled in Christ,

   - No remaining debt,

   - Corruption removed,

   - Love perfected,

   - Will permanently aligned with God,

   - Kept by Father, Son, and Spirit,

   - Irreversible union.


Failure is impossible, not because freedom is erased, but because corruption is removed
and communion is perfected.

The difference is ontological confirmation, not merely expanded information.


#### V. Reprobation Within a Christ-Centered Reality
We affirm that reprobation is not a co-equal decretal symmetry with election.

Reprobation is:

   - Judicial consequence,

   - Alienation within a Christ-ordered creation,

   - The persistence of corruption apart from union with Christ.


Election is active mercy.
Non-election is the withholding of effectual rescue.
Condemnation remains judicial.

Light does not require darkness.
Darkness cannot abide light.
#### VI. Trinitarian Harmony
We affirm distinct personal roles within the one Triune God:

   - The Father as source and giver,

   - The Son as covenant head and mediator,

   - The Spirit as personal agent of communion and participation.


In redemptive mode, the Spirit regenerates and seals.
 In telos mode, the Spirit perfects and confirms communion.

The Trinity acts inseparably in will, yet personally in order.


#### VII. The Governing Conclusion
The Fall did not create the goal.
It altered the path to the goal.

Redemption is contingent.
Union is ultimate.

Christ is not defined by sin.
Sin is resolved within Christ’s eternal purpose.

The final state is not Eden restored,
but communion confirmed.


Closing Statement
This declaration seeks to preserve:

   - The primacy of Christ,

   - The coherence of divine justice,

   - The asymmetry of mercy and judgment,
   - The integrity of Trinitarian roles,

   - The stability of confirmed glory.


Christ is the telos.
History serves Him.
Redemption magnifies Him.
Glory confirms Him.

Designation: 999


Clause: Non-Election and Judicial Condemnation (Asymmetry Preserved)

     We affirm that all who are not united to Christ remain in Adam and therefore remain
     truly guilty and legally condemned. Condemnation is judicial and creature-grounded
     (Adamic guilt and voluntary sin), not a divine manufacture of evil.

     We affirm that election is God’s active rescue through effectual calling, resulting in
     union with Christ and the application of the Cross in time.

     We affirm that non-election is not a positive decree of sin, nor an efficient causation
     of unbelief; it is God’s decision not to grant effectual rescue, leaving the non-elect in
     the state they already possess in Adam, under just judgment.

     Therefore: Election is active mercy; non-election is the withholding of
     effectual mercy; condemnation remains judicial.

     Guardrail: Decree ≠ application, and decree ≠ efficient causation of sin.
