# **DOCTRINE OF THE WILES OF THE DEVIL**

*(Indiana Mode A — Structural Formulation)*

---

## **I. Definition**

From Epistle to the Ephesians 6:11:

“the wiles of the devil”

We define:

**The wiles of the devil are structured, repeatable strategies of distortion applied to human perception, desire, and authority in order to redirect participation away from true mediation and toward counterfeit systems.**

---

## **II. Foundational Constraints**

This doctrine operates under non-negotiables:

---

### **C1 — Mediation Exclusivity**

* Access to life is singular  
* Cannot be supplemented or bypassed

---

### **C2 — Human Agency**

* Humans act  
* Humans are responsible

---

### **C3 — Adversary Limitation**

* Not sovereign  
* Cannot create life  
* Cannot directly control

---

### **C4 — System Pressure**

* Systems tend toward:  
  * externalization  
  * control  
  * participation routing

---

## **III. The Three Primary Wiles (Core Structure)**

All wiles reduce to three operational vectors:

---

## **1\. Distortion of Desire (Temptation)**

### **Function:**

Redirect desire away from mediated dependence toward self-satisfaction

---

### **Biblical Pattern:**

* “turn stones to bread”

---

### **Structural Form:**

* Immediate gratification  
* Self-provision  
* Independence from mediation

---

### **System Expression:**

* consumption systems  
* survival-driven compromise  
* “ends justify means”

---

---

## **2\. Distortion of Truth (Deception)**

### **Function:**

Reframe reality so that false paths appear legitimate

---

### **Biblical Pattern:**

* “throw yourself down” (Scripture twisted)

---

### **Structural Form:**

* partial truth  
* misapplied truth  
* redefined trust

---

### **System Expression:**

* doctrinal drift  
* ideological systems  
* narrative manipulation

---

---

## **3\. Distortion of Authority (Counterfeit Kingship)**

### **Function:**

Offer control, power, or influence apart from mediation

---

### **Biblical Pattern:**

* “all the kingdoms of the world”

---

### **Structural Form:**

* authority without submission  
* rule without mediation  
* control through system

---

### **System Expression:**

* empire  
* coercive religion  
* political-religious fusion

---

---

# **IV. Unified Mechanism**

All three wiles operate together:

**Desire is redirected → Truth is distorted → Authority is seized**

This produces:

**Counterfeit participation systems**

# **VIII. Boundary Conditions**

This doctrine DOES NOT claim:

* omnipresent control  
* direct authorship of events  
* independent system creation

---

It DOES claim:

**Consistent directional distortion applied to systems that already must form**

---

# **IX. Final Compression**

**The wiles of the devil are the structured distortions of desire, truth, and authority that redirect human participation away from true mediation and into externally stabilized systems of counterfeit identity, which, when scaled, converge into totalizing adversarial structures.**
