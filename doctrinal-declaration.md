## PART I — ONE-PAGE DOCTRINAL
# DECLARATION
(Concise, Readable, Statement-Level)

The Christ-Centered Arc of Creation

We affirm that creation exists through Christ and for Christ.
 The Incarnation was not a reaction to the Fall, but the unveiling of the eternal telos embedded
in creation itself.

Humanity was created good, relationally aligned, and probationary.
Alignment was not mechanical but covenantal, and therefore rupture was possible. Through
deception and trust displacement, humanity fractured its telos, resulting in cascading corruption:
mortality, futility, and conflict.

The curse was not arbitrary wrath but the judicial and ontological consequence of severed
communion.

Yet immediately, God declared the counter-trajectory:
the seed would crush the serpent.

From that moment, history became redemptive.
The Fall altered the pathway but not the destination.

In Christ, God entered the corridor of corruption, bore judgment, defeated death, and disarmed
rebellion. The resurrection inaugurated a new humanity—incorruptible and confirmed.

Glorification does not return us to Eden.
It elevates us beyond Eden.

The final state is not vulnerable innocence but unrupturable communion.
Freedom is not removed but perfected—desire healed, corruption gone, alignment confirmed.

The Fall was not logically necessary for God.
 But it was permitted and incorporated into a plan that reveals a fullness of divine glory—justice,
mercy, patience, covenant faithfulness, and victory over evil—that untested innocence alone
could not display.

Creation was soil.
History was cultivation.
Christ is the harvest.
Glorification is the consummation.
