# **PART I — THE SOURCE LAYER**

## **Jesus Hermeneutic Lens**

This is the **root interpretive system** from which the apostolic hermeneutics derive.

Jesus demonstrates how Scripture should be read through **ten structural interpretive beams**.

---

# **Core Interpretive Beams**

### **1\. Canonical Christ Witness**

All Scripture bears witness to Christ.

Examples:

* Luke 24  
* John 5

Structure:

Scripture  
→ witness  
→ Messiah.

---

### **2\. Prophetic Fulfillment Recognition**

Prophetic texts describe the Messiah’s mission and work.

Example:

Luke 4

Isaiah prophecy  
→ proclamation  
→ liberation  
→ restoration.

---

### **3\. Suffering → Glory Necessity**

The Messiah must suffer before vindication.

Example:

Luke 24\.

Structure:

suffering  
→ resurrection  
→ glory.

---

### **4\. Textual Depth Reasoning**

Scriptural wording and structure contain deeper implications.

Examples:

Psalm 110 (Matthew 22\)  
Exodus 3 (Sadducees debate).

---

### **5\. Creation Design Priority**

Creation establishes the baseline for interpreting covenant law.

Example:

Matthew 19\.

Creation  
→ covenant law interpreted through it.

---

### **6\. Institutional Fulfillment Supremacy**

Covenant institutions point toward greater realities fulfilled in Christ.

Example:

Matthew 12\.

Temple → Christ  
Sabbath → Christ.

---

### **7\. Covenant Concession Recognition**

Certain covenant laws reflect concession to human hardness.

Example:

Matthew 19\.

Divorce law → concession, not design.

---

### **8\. Interpretive Illumination vs Blindness**

Understanding Scripture requires receptive hearts.

Examples:

* Luke 24  
* John 5  
* Matthew 13\.

---

### **9\. Revelation Governance**

Jesus controls how revelation is disclosed.

Example:

Matthew 13\.

Parables both reveal and conceal truth.

---

### **10\. Historical Typological Fulfillment**

Earlier historical events foreshadow greater fulfillment in Christ.

Example:

Jonah → resurrection sign.

---

# **PART II — APOSTOLIC DEPLOYMENT LENSES**

The apostles do not invent new hermeneutics.

They **deploy specific beams of Jesus’ hermeneutic system**.

---

# **Pauline Lens**

(Covenant Architecture)

Primary interpretive beams used:

* Canonical Christ Witness  
* Creation Design Priority  
* Textual Depth Reasoning  
* Historical Typology

Focus:

* covenant structure  
* federal headship  
* promise fulfillment.

Key passages:

Romans 5  
Galatians 3  
1 Corinthians 10\.

---

# **Petrine Lens**

(Prophetic Convergence)

Primary interpretive beams used:

* Prophetic Fulfillment  
* Suffering → Glory pattern  
* Historical Typology  
* Canonical Witness

Focus:

* prophetic witness  
* covenant identity  
* suffering Messiah.

Key passages:

Acts 2  
1 Peter 1  
1 Peter 2\.

---

# **Johannine Lens**

(Sign and Symbol Architecture)

Primary interpretive beams used:

* Canonical Christ Witness  
* Revelation Governance  
* Historical Typology  
* Present Fulfillment

Focus:

* signs  
* incarnational symbolism  
* revelation through narrative.

Key passages:

John 6  
John 7  
John 11\.

# **PART III — CHRISTOLOGICAL OFFICE INTEGRATION**

This layer explains **why the apostles emphasize different interpretive aspects**.

Christ holds **three biblical offices**.

These offices correspond closely to the apostolic lenses.

---

# **Prophet Office**

Christ reveals God’s word and prophetic fulfillment.

Corresponding apostolic lens:

**Peter**

Key themes:

* prophetic witness  
* fulfillment  
* suffering Messiah.

---

# **Priest Office**

Christ mediates covenant redemption and sacrifice.

Corresponding apostolic lens:

**Paul**

Key themes:

* covenant structure  
* atonement  
* federal headship.

---

# **King Office**

Christ reveals and governs the kingdom.

Corresponding apostolic lens:

**John**

Key themes:

* kingdom signs  
* revelation  
* divine authority.

# **PART IV — APOSTOLIC HERMENEUTIC STRESS MATRIX**

This matrix converts the framework into a **working interpretive tool**.

The matrix evaluates difficult passages through **all four lenses simultaneously**.

---

# **Step 1 — Identify Canonical Anchor**

Determine:

* Old Testament background  
* covenant structures  
* prophetic context.

---

# **Step 2 — Apply Jesus Lens**

Ask:

Which of the ten interpretive beams are active?

Examples:

* typology  
* fulfillment  
* creation priority  
* institutional fulfillment.

---

# **Step 3 — Apply Apostolic Lenses**

Evaluate the passage through:

Pauline lens  
Petrine lens  
Johannine lens.

Each highlights different structural elements.

---

# **Step 4 — Convergence Test**

Interpretation succeeds if the lenses converge on:

* Christ’s identity  
* Christ’s mission  
* Christ’s kingdom.
