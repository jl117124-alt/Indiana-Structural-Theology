# **SET IV — CONVERGENCE ARCHITECTURE**

# **VOLUME IV.5 — TERMINAL IDENTIFICATION**

*(Canonical Form — Resolution Layer)*

---

## **I. Purpose**

To establish:

**The condition under which identity is resolved once all prior constraints are fully satisfied**

---

## **II. Boundary**

This document assumes:

* Set I is complete  
* Set II is complete  
* Set III is complete  
* IV.0 interface conditions are defined  
* IV.1 independence is established  
* IV.2 constraint alignment is established  
* IV.3 bridging necessity is established  
* IV.4 identification constraint is established

---

This document does NOT:

* introduce new constraints  
* relax any prior constraint  
* permit approximation or partial satisfaction

---

It establishes only:

**the condition for final identification**

---

## **III. Definition — Terminal Identification**

Terminal identification occurs when:

**a single subject uniquely satisfies all prior constraints without contradiction, ambiguity, or competing candidates**

---

## **IV. Conditions for Identification**

A subject is identified if and only if:

---

### **IV.1 Structural Satisfaction**

The subject satisfies:

* all requirements of Set I  
* all requirements of Set II

---

### **IV.2 Bridging Satisfaction**

The subject satisfies:

* all conditions required by IV.3 (bridging necessity)

---

### **IV.3 Constraint Integrity**

The subject:

* does not violate any prior constraint  
* does not require reinterpretation of any prior result

---

### **IV.4 Referential Determinacy**

The subject is:

* a single identifiable referent  
* not a class, abstraction, or category

---

### **IV.5 Exclusivity**

No other candidate:

* satisfies all constraints equally

---

### **IV.6 Continuity**

The subject remains:

* the same subject across all required operations  
* without identity fragmentation or substitution

---

## **V. Failure Conditions**

Identification does NOT occur if:

---

### **V.1 Multiple Candidates**

More than one subject satisfies the full constraint set

---

### **V.2 Constraint Violation**

Any subject fails to satisfy even one constraint

---

### **V.3 Ambiguity**

The subject is not uniquely specified

---

### **V.4 Discontinuity**

The subject is not the same across all required operations

---

## **VI. Result**

When all conditions are satisfied:

* identity is resolved  
* the solution space collapses to a single subject  
* no further candidates remain

---

## **VII. Boundary of IV.5**

This document:

* does not assert that any specific subject has been identified  
* does not introduce or evaluate any particular claim  
* does not move beyond constraint-based identification

---

It establishes only:

**the condition under which identification is complete**

---

## **VIII. Final Statement of Set IV**

Set IV establishes:

* IV.0 → interaction without resolution  
* IV.1 → independence without circularity  
* IV.2 → alignment without reduction  
* IV.3 → necessity of bridging  
* IV.4 → constraints on valid subject  
* IV.5 → condition for identification

---

## **IX. Locked Statement**

**Identity is resolved only when a single subject uniquely satisfies all independently derived constraints, fulfills the required bridging conditions, and does so without contradiction, ambiguity, or competition.**
