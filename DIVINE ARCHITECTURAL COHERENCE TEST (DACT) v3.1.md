# **DIVINE ARCHITECTURAL COHERENCE TEST (DACT) v3.1**

**Canonical Structural Integrity Framework**

---

## **STATUS**

Operational  
Indiana Mode compliant  
Constraint-based  
Non-prophetic  
Non-denominational  
Closure-aware (Revelation bounded)

---

# **PURPOSE**

To evaluate any claimed revelation (text, tradition, apparition, doctrinal development) for **structural coherence with the canonical architecture of Scripture**, centered in Christ, bounded by non-contradiction, and restricted to the completed revelation delivered through the apostolic witness.

---

# **DECISION RULE**

A single **hard FAIL** under a non-negotiable constraint disqualifies divine origin unless the contradiction is shown to be only apparent without rewriting prior commitments.

---

# **OUTPUT TYPES**

* **CANONICAL** — Structurally necessary, apostolic, non-derivative, revelatory  
* **SECONDARY** — Structurally coherent but non-foundational (interpretive, derivative)  
* **FAIL** — Hard structural contradiction  
* **INDETERMINATE** — Insufficient data

---

# **PART I — FOUNDATIONAL NON-NEGOTIABLES**

### **1\. Divine Veracity Constraint**

God does not lie, deceive in moral essence, or contradict Himself.

**Fail if:**

* God is portrayed as essentially duplicitous  
* Truth and falsehood are equally attributed to Him

---

### **2\. Divine Moral Coherence Constraint**

God’s moral attributes must not be self-contradictory.

**Fail if:**

* Good and evil are both essential to God  
* Justice and injustice are equally grounded in Him

---

### **3\. Telos Continuity Constraint**

All revelation must cohere with the established redemptive telos culminating in Christ.

**Fail if:**

* The final purpose of creation is reversed  
* Christ is displaced as climactic fulfillment  
* Redemption is replaced by an alternate end-state

---

### **4\. Non-Contradiction of Fulfillment Constraint**

Progressive revelation may clarify but may not deny completed fulfillment.

**Fail if:**

* A fulfilled covenant act is declared false  
* Christ’s finished work is functionally negated

---

# **PART II — COVENANT ARCHITECTURE CONSTRAINTS**

### **5\. Federal Representation Integrity**

Adam–Christ federal symmetry must remain intact.

**Fail if:**

* Imputation is denied  
* Representation collapses  
* Justification becomes self-derived

---

### **6\. Justice Satisfied ≠ Justification Applied**

The Cross satisfies justice once-for-all.  
Application occurs in time at union.

**Fail if:**

* Eternal justification is introduced  
* Application is backdated pre-union  
* Penal satisfaction is duplicated

---

### **7\. No Parallel Soteriology Constraint**

No alternative salvation mechanism may operate alongside Christ’s atonement.

**Fail if:**

* Ritual, money, works, priesthood, angelic mediation, or human effort function as independent salvific systems

---

# **PART III — DEATH CORRIDOR FINALITY**

### **8\. Death-State Finality Constraint**

Sequence:  
Life → Death → Immediate Sorting → Intermediate State → Resurrection → Judgment → Final State

Death fixes legal-moral standing.

**Fail if:**

* Post-mortem actions alter the dead’s moral state  
* Sacrifice is applied after death  
* A secondary corridor is introduced

---

### **9\. Christ-Exclusive Jurisdiction Clause**

Only Christ holds authority over Death and Hades.

**Fail if:**

* Humans or other agents can redeem, extract, purify, or relocate the dead

---

### **10\. No Mid-Corridor Mechanism**

Between death and resurrection there is:

* No sacrificial extension  
* No ritual economy  
* No transactional system

**Fail if:**  
Any effective post-death adjustment mechanism is introduced

---

# **PART IV — NARRATIVE INTEGRITY**

### **11\. Narrative Non-Bypass Constraint**

Narrative must not resolve covenant rupture through human technique.

**Fail if:**

* Moral performance purges sin  
* Death is defeated by ordinary piety  
* Mediation is replaced by human or angelic function

---

# **PART V — HOSTILE RECOVERABILITY**

### **Rule**

Revelation must survive:

* Memory fracture  
* Cultural collapse  
* Transmission compression

**Fail if:**

* Requires hidden or elite knowledge  
* Cannot be reconstructed under loss conditions

---

# **PART VI — INTERPRETIVE DISCIPLINE**

### **12\. Parable vs Ontology Discipline**

Symbolic language must not negate real structural claims.

**Fail if:**

* Symbolism is used to justify contradiction  
* Ontological meaning collapses under interpretation

---

# **PART VII — STRUCTURAL VALIDATION (V3 CORE ADDITIONS)**

---

### **13\. Apostolic Proximity Constraint**

Canonical revelation must arise within or directly from the apostolic witness.

**Fail or downgrade if:**

* Late emergence  
* No apostolic grounding  
* No early authoritative circulation

---

### **14\. Dependency Direction Constraint**

Revelation must be foundational, not derivative.

**Fail if:**

* Text depends on prior revelation  
* Reinterprets rather than originates  
* Functions as a downstream remix

---

### **15\. Authority Density Classification**

Every text must be classified:

* **Canonical** — Load-bearing, non-redundant  
* **Secondary** — Coherent but dependent  
* **Rejected** — Contradictory

---

### **16\. Non-Superfluity Constraint (Canonical Necessity Rule)**

A canonical text must be structurally necessary.

**Fail as canonical if:**

* It introduces no new structural necessity  
* It is removable without loss of:  
  * doctrinal clarity  
  * redemptive progression  
  * architectural integrity

---

# **PART VIII — REVELATION CLOSURE (V3.1 ADDITION)**

---

### **17\. Revelation Closure Constraint**

Revelation is bounded to the redemptive-historical event of Christ and the apostolic witness.

No new revelation may introduce previously unrevealed redemptive structure after this closure.

---

**Fail if:**

* A text claims new divine revelation after the apostolic period  
* New doctrine is introduced as originating from God rather than derived from Scripture  
* A new mediator, mechanism, or authority is presented  
* Previously unrevealed salvific structure is added

---

**Allow:**

* Exposition  
* Clarification  
* Theological synthesis  
* Doctrinal development derived from existing revelation

---

# **PART IX — FINAL INTEGRATION TEST**

A canonical text must simultaneously:

1. Preserve Christ as exclusive mediator  
2. Preserve once-for-all atonement  
3. Preserve death-state finality  
4. Preserve resurrection sequence  
5. Preserve final judgment integrity  
6. Introduce no secondary redemption system  
7. Be apostolically grounded  
8. Be non-derivative  
9. Be structurally necessary  
10. Belong to the closed revelation window

---

# **FAIL CONDITION**

A single violation of a non-negotiable constraint \= **FAIL**

---

# **FINAL FORMULATION**

Canon is not created by declaration.

Canon is what remains when all contradiction, derivation, redundancy, and post-apostolic expansion are removed—leaving only that which is structurally necessary to the redemptive work of God in Christ.

---

# **SHORT FORM (FIELD USE)**

* Does it contradict? → FAIL  
* Does it introduce another way of salvation? → FAIL  
* Does it alter death or judgment? → FAIL  
* Is it post-apostolic revelation? → FAIL  
* Is it derivative? → NOT CANONICAL  
* Is it unnecessary? → NOT CANONICAL  
* Does it carry the structure? → CANONICAL
