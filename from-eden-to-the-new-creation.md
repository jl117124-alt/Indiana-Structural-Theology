From Eden to the New Creation
The Mirror Between Genesis 1–3 and Revelation 21–22
Genesis begins the story.
Revelation completes it.

The end of the Bible intentionally mirrors the beginning. What was lost in Genesis is
restored—and even surpassed—through Jesus Christ.


1. Creation → New Creation
Beginning

Genesis 1:1

      “In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.”

God creates the universe as good and ordered.


Ending

Revelation 21:1

      “Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth.”

The story does not end with escape from creation.

God renews creation itself.

Christ restores the world that sin damaged.


2. The Garden → The City-Garden
Beginning

Genesis 2:8–10

God plants a garden called Eden.

It contains:

    - the tree of life
    - a river flowing through it
    - the dwelling place of humanity with God


Ending

Revelation 22:1–2

The new creation contains:

    - the river of life
    - the tree of life
    - the dwelling place of humanity with God

But now it is not just a garden.

It is a garden-city.

The restored creation is greater than Eden.


3. God Walking with Humanity → God
Dwelling with Humanity
Beginning

Genesis 3:8

      “They heard the sound of the LORD God walking in the garden.”

God’s presence was near and personal.
Ending

Revelation 21:3

        “Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man.”

The distance created by sin is completely removed.

What was interrupted in Eden becomes permanent in the new creation.


4. The Curse → The Curse Removed
Beginning of the Curse

Genesis 3:17

        “Cursed is the ground because of you.”

Creation becomes marked by:

   -    suffering
   -    death
   -    toil
   -    pain


End of the Curse

Revelation 22:3

        “No longer will there be anything accursed.”

The curse introduced in Genesis is completely removed through Christ.


5. Access to the Tree of Life Lost →
Access Restored
Beginning

Genesis 3:24

      “He drove out the man… to guard the way to the tree of life.”

Humanity is exiled from the source of life.


Ending

Revelation 22:14

      “Blessed are those who wash their robes, so that they may have the right to the tree
      of life.”

What was guarded in Genesis is freely accessible in the new creation.


6. Death Enters → Death Is Destroyed
Beginning

Genesis 3:19

      “For you are dust, and to dust you shall return.”

Death becomes humanity’s destiny.


Ending

Revelation 21:4

      “Death shall be no more.”

Christ defeats the final enemy.
7. Marriage in Eden → The Marriage of the
Lamb
Beginning

Genesis 2:24

Marriage is established between Adam and Eve.


Ending

Revelation 19:7

      “The marriage of the Lamb has come.”

Human marriage becomes a shadow of the greater union between Christ and His people.


8. Adam’s Failure → Christ’s Victory
Beginning

Adam fails the covenant command.

Genesis 3:6

Sin enters the world.


Ending

Christ succeeds where Adam failed.

1 Corinthians 15:22

      “As in Adam all die, so also in Christ shall all be made alive.”

Christ becomes the Second Adam who restores humanity.
The Full Story of the Bible
The Bible moves through four stages:

    Stage           Scripture

 Creation      Genesis 1–2

 Fall          Genesis 3

 Redemptio     The rest of the Bible
 n

 Restoration   Revelation 21–22

Genesis shows the world as it was meant to be.

Revelation shows the world as it will be through Christ.

Everything in between tells the story of how God accomplishes that restoration.


Final Teaching Point for Your Study
When we read Genesis, we are not just reading the beginning of a story.

We are reading the opening blueprint of the entire Bible.

Genesis shows us:

   - what humanity was created for
   - what was lost through sin
   - and why the world needs a Savior

Revelation shows us:

   - what Christ restores
   - what the new creation will look like
   - and how God’s plan finally reaches completion.
The story that begins in Eden ends in a greater Eden.

And the one who brings it to completion is Jesus Christ.
