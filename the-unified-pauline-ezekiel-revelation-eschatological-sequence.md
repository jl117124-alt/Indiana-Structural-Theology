The Unified Pauline–Ezekiel–Revelation
Eschatological Sequence
(Clean Structural Order Without Timeline Speculation)
This document lays out the full redemptive sequence using:

   - Paul’s explicit ordering (Romans, Corinthians, Thessalonians)
   - Ezekiel’s restoration–Gog pattern
   - Revelation 20’s end-horizon structure

This is sequence, not calendar.


#### I. Present Reign of Christ
   1. Christ is enthroned now.
          ○ He reigns until all enemies are placed under His feet (1 Cor 15:25).
          ○ The kingdom is active, not postponed.
   2. Satan is restrained in a specific sense.
          ○ The nations are no longer held in total darkness.
          ○ The gospel advances globally.

This corresponds to Revelation 20:1–6 (millennial phase = church age).


#### II. Gospel Expansion Phase
   3. The gospel spreads to the nations.
          ○ Gentiles are grafted into the olive tree (Romans 11).
          ○ The church expands across the world.
   4. A partial hardening remains on ethnic Israel.
          ○ Not total.
          ○ Not permanent.
          ○ Judicial but temporary (Romans 11:25).
This entire phase runs throughout the church age.


#### III. Late-Stage Fulness Phase
   5. The “fullness of the Gentiles” comes in.
           ○ This is a completion marker, not a date.
           ○ It signals the nearing of the end-horizon.
   6. A significant mercy toward ethnic Israel occurs.
           ○ “All Israel will be saved” (Romans 11).
           ○ Paul calls it “life from the dead.”
           ○ This is spiritual grafting back into the olive tree.
           ○ It is not bodily resurrection.
           ○ It is not geopolitical restoration.
           ○ It is covenant restoration in Christ.

Ezekiel 37 provides the prophetic imagery grammar for this:
Dry bones → Spirit breath → restored covenant people.

This occurs near the end of the church age,
but before the final collapse.


#### IV. Final Intensification
   7. Rebellion intensifies.
          ○ “Man of lawlessness” revealed (2 Thessalonians 2).
          ○ Deception increases.
          ○ Hostility toward the saints sharpens.
   8. Satan is released (Revelation 20:7).
          ○ Gog and Magog pattern emerges.
          ○ Final global opposition to the people of God.
          ○ This echoes Ezekiel 38–39. 

This is the last concentrated rebellion.
#### V. The Parousia (Single Climactic Event)
   9. Christ returns.
          ○ The Lord descends (1 Thess 4).
          ○ He destroys the lawless one (2 Thess 2).
          ○ Gog-pattern rebellion is ended (Rev 20:9–10).
   10. Resurrection occurs at His coming.
          ○ Dead in Christ rise.
          ○ Believers transformed (1 Cor 15).
          ○ There is not a separate, earlier bodily resurrection event.


#### VI. Final Judgment
   11. The Great White Throne appears (Rev 20:11).
   12. All the dead are judged.
   13. Death and Hades are thrown into the lake of fire.

This fulfills:
“The last enemy to be destroyed is death.” (1 Cor 15:26)

There is no extended historical era after this.


#### VII. Consummation
   14. The kingdom is delivered to the Father (1 Cor 15:24).
   15. God dwells with His people permanently (Rev 21–22).
   16. The curse is gone.
   17. Death is abolished.
   18. God hides His face no more.

This fulfills the covenant-restoration telos anticipated in Ezekiel.


Summary Sequence (Condensed Form)
 1. Christ reigns.
 2. Gospel spreads to nations.
 3. Jewish partial hardening continues.
 4. Gentile fullness reached.
 5. Jewish grafting mercy (“life from the dead”).
 6. Final rebellion intensifies (lawlessness / Gog).
 7. Parousia.
 8. Resurrection.
 9. Final judgment.
 10. Death destroyed.
 11. New creation.


What This Framework Avoids
 -   No double bodily resurrections.
 -   No political millennial kingdom.
 -   No literal future dry-bones corpse event.
 -   No Jewish salvation after the parousia.
 -   No timeline speculation.
 -   No collapse of covenant restoration into ethnic nationalism.


What This Framework Preserves
 -   Paul’s ordering constraints.
 -   Romans 11’s Jewish mercy promise.
 -   1 Corinthians 15’s single resurrection event.
 -   Thessalonian parousia unity.
 -   Ezekiel’s restoration–Gog pattern.
 -   Revelation 20’s end-horizon sequence.
 -   Christ-centered telos.
